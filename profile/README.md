## Description

Welcome to **[Liberu Real Estate](https://www.liberusoftware.com)**, our revolutionary open-source project that reshapes the real estate industry. Built by [Liberu Software](https://www.liberusoftware.com) using **Laravel 12**, **PHP 8.5**, **Livewire 3**, and **Filament 5**, Liberu Real Estate is not just a software solution for property management — it's a dynamic platform designed to empower real estate professionals, property owners, and investors with innovative tools and seamless workflows. It supports integrations with **Rightmove**, **Zoopla**, and **OnTheMarket**.

**Key Features:**

1. **Intuitive Property Management:** Manage real estate properties with ease. From listings and transactions to tenant relationships, our project simplifies the complexities of property management, ensuring a streamlined and efficient experience.

2. **Dynamic Livewire Interactions:** Real-time interactions powered by Livewire 3. Update property details, manage transactions, and communicate seamlessly with clients without page refreshes.

3. **Efficient Admin Panel:** Filament 5 provides administrators with powerful tools to manage property listings, customise settings, and oversee the entire real estate ecosystem.

4. **Transaction Tracking and Reporting:** Comprehensive tracking and reporting features to monitor property sales, leases, and financial transactions, ensuring accurate and up-to-date financial records.

5. **Customisable Listings and Search:** Tailor property listings and search functionalities to match your real estate business's unique needs.

Liberu Real Estate is open source, released under the permissive MIT licence. We invite real estate professionals, developers, and property management teams to contribute to its evolution.
